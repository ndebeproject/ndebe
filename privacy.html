The project / Privacy

Privacy policy

Updated 26 September 2026

About this policy

The Ńdébé Project (“we”, “us”) provides information and tools for learning and using the Ńdébé script. This policy describes the new Ndebe website preview at ndebeproject.github.io/ndebe/. It does not describe the separate Type Ndebe website or the existing website currently at ndebe.org.

For privacy questions or requests, email messages@ndebe.org.

Information used when you visit

GitHub Pages hosts this preview. GitHub receives technical information needed to deliver pages and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Its privacy statement explains its processing.

You can read the site without creating an account. Searches and learning-tool inputs are processed in your browser. Our analytics does not collect their contents. Following an external link, sending a contact message or using a separate website has its own data flow.

Page-view analytics

We use PostHog US Cloud to understand which pages are visited. The configuration sends the page’s fixed address, technical library information and a browser user agent. PostHog processes the connection IP address and user agent to calculate a daily identifier, then removes those two values from the stored event. These counts are estimates rather than a record of named visitors.

This analytics configuration uses no cookies or browser storage. It does not capture clicks, session recordings, form contents, learning-tool inputs, search terms, referring-page addresses, or the query strings and fragments in page addresses. It does not create personal profiles. We skip analytics when your browser signals Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. This privacy-policy page does not load analytics.

Learn more in PostHog’s privacy policy and its explanation of cookieless measurement.

When you contact us

The contact form asks for your name, email address, subject and message. A telephone number is optional. We use this information to receive and respond to your enquiry, including questions about the script or licensing. Please do not include passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for your enquiry.

Cloudflare processes form requests and Resend delivers the message to messages@ndebe.org. Our email-forwarding service, provided through Squarespace, and Google Gmail process the message for delivery to our inbox. Your email address is used as the reply-to address. You can also email us directly.

Cloudflare Turnstile checks browser and connection signals to help distinguish visitors from automated abuse. The form also uses the connection IP address to limit rapid submissions. The form’s application code does not deliberately log your message or contact fields; hosting and delivery providers may retain operational records needed to provide and protect their services.

Provider information: Cloudflare privacy policy, Turnstile privacy notice, Resend privacy policy, Squarespace privacy policy, and Google privacy policy.

Cookies and advertising

Advertising is currently disabled on this preview. It does not load Google AdSense or an advertising consent message. Our cookieless page analytics is separate from any future advertising choices.

We plan to support the website with Google AdSense. When advertising is enabled, Google and other participating advertising providers may use cookies and similar technologies to show, measure and protect ads. Personalized advertising can use earlier visits to this and other websites. We will update this policy and provide the applicable consent or opt-out controls before enabling that setup.

You can manage Google’s personalized-ad preferences through My Ad Center. Google explains how it uses information from partner sites. A browser’s cookie controls also let you manage stored cookies; these controls are distinct from the site’s advertising-consent choices.

Why we use information and who receives it

We use information to operate and secure the website, understand page usage, answer enquiries and handle licensing discussions. The providers named above receive information for those functions. We do not use contact messages for advertising profiles. Information may also need to be disclosed where required by law or to protect legal rights.

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, these activities may rely on our legitimate interests in running and protecting the site and answering enquiries, steps requested before entering a contract, or legal obligations. Advertising that requires consent will rely on the choice made through the relevant controls.

Retention and international processing

Contact correspondence may remain in our mailbox while an enquiry is being handled and where needed for an ongoing relationship, licensing records, legal obligations or resolving disputes. Retention depends on that purpose; this policy does not promise an automatic deletion deadline. You can ask us to delete correspondence that is no longer needed.

Analytics records and provider operational records are subject to the services’ retention settings and policies. A daily analytics identifier changing does not mean the underlying page-view records are deleted daily. Our providers may process information in the United States and other countries outside your own. Their privacy notices describe their international processing and safeguards.

Your choices and requests

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal information, restrict or object to its use, and withdraw consent where processing depends on consent. Email messages@ndebe.org with your request. We may need enough information to verify and locate the relevant records; we may be unable to associate cookieless visit counts with a particular person.

You may also contact the data-protection authority in your jurisdiction. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing that was lawful before withdrawal. This site’s current Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control handling applies to our analytics; it does not stop hosting or contact-security processing needed to deliver those services.

Young learners and external sites

The learning material can be used by young learners. They do not need to send us personal information to read it. Parents or guardians can contact us about information a child may have submitted. Links to Type Ndebe, social networks and other external sites take you to services with their own privacy practices.

Updates

We will update this page as the website’s services change and show the revision date above.
